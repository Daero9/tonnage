2. Who it is for

Tonnup is for people aged 16 or older. Its calculations rely on formulas established for adults, and weight tracking is not suitable for a younger audience.

3. What the app does, and what it does not do

Tonnup helps you organise your strength workouts, track your loads, weight, cardio sessions and what you eat, and draw benchmarks from them: calorie needs, protein goal, loads to aim for, progress.

Tonnup is not a medical device and gives no medical, dietary or personalised coaching advice. The values shown are estimates:

  • calorie needs and the protein goal come from statistical formulas — a person's reality can differ markedly;
  • food values come from an average composition table, from Open Food Facts (entered by the community and by brands, with possible errors) or from the automatic analysis of a photo, which recognises and weighs “by eye”: these are flagged as estimated and can be corrected before being saved;
  • suggested loads are calculated from your previous workouts; they do not take into account pain, fatigue or an injury the app does not know about.

You remain the sole judge of what you do with these benchmarks. If you have a medical condition, an injury, an eating disorder, are pregnant or are under treatment, seek medical advice before following the app's indications. Do not go beyond a load you cannot control, and stop if you feel pain.
